# Forge Watch Collection — Galaxy Watch 8

Ten original Wear OS / Watch Face Format faces branded **FORGE**.

## Forge Signature — elegant analog series
All five use true analog hour/minute/seconds hands, an AOD-specific low-power presentation, restrained typography, large tap/complication zones where appropriate, and FORGE as the dial marque at 12 o'clock. The design language is luxury-inspired but uses original Forge geometry and assets rather than copying a specific commercial dial.

1. **Forge Sovereign** — black lacquer dial, warm-gold indices/hands, date at 3, minute track, discreet power reserve/battery complication. AOD: black + outline hands/indices + FORGE.
2. **Forge Slate** — charcoal sunburst-style geometry, polished silver baton indices, blue seconds accent, day/date at 3. AOD: monochrome silver essentials.
3. **Forge Ivory** — warm ivory dial, black Roman numerals at cardinal positions, thin feuille-inspired original hands, small seconds treatment. AOD: black background with ivory essentials to protect OLED.
4. **Forge Emerald** — deep green dial, silver furniture, geometric fluted perimeter motif, date magnifier-inspired *layout* without copying Rolex artwork. AOD: black with green accent minute markers.
5. **Forge Executive** — midnight/navy dial, clean applied baton layout, dual lower complications for battery + next event, subtle red seconds tip. AOD: minimal hands, cardinal indices, FORGE.

## Forge Pulse — unique digital series
All five expose steps prominently, support color configuration, have oversized touch targets, and retain an efficient AOD.

6. **Forge Pulse** — huge stacked HH/MM, circular step progress ring, date, battery; cyan/lime/orange/pink/purple themes.
7. **Forge Blocks** — four modular tiles: time, steps, battery, date/weather complication; selectable high-contrast palettes.
8. **Forge Orbit** — central digital time surrounded by step-progress orbit, battery arc and day/date; neon accent themes.
9. **Forge Split** — vertically split hours/minutes with oversized numerals, steps bar across bottom, two configurable complication buttons.
10. **Forge Spectrum** — restrained black base with user-selectable multi-accent rings, time center, steps + goal percentage, battery and date.

## Interaction baseline
- Large complication/tap targets (approximately 100–130 px on 450x450 reference canvas).
- Analog: date/calendar, battery, next event, weather/health provider slots depending on face.
- Digital: steps is visually first-class plus battery/date and at least two customizable complication slots where layout permits.
- Ambient/AOD removes decorative fills and seconds motion, minimizes lit pixels, and retains legible time + FORGE branding.
- No third-party logos or copied proprietary artwork/fonts.
